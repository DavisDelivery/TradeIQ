What to build next, and what to refuse to build

23 agents across six benchmark tracks, five code audits and three integration studies produced 164 findings. Every top recommendation was then handed to a skeptical engineer told to disprove it. All eight were challenged — which is the most useful result in here.

The headline

Three verified defects outranked every feature idea

Real broker fills wrote a journal schema no client reader understood, so Positions and Base Rates were blank for every trade actually placed. ChartView fired a Claude call on tab open, violating the on-demand rule. And light — the default theme, not the "hardcoded dark" I briefed the researchers with — renders gains below WCAG AA.

Fix those, then make the phone usable, before adding a single new signal, board or vendor.

Ranked roadmap

Ordered by value to one solo trader on an iPhone with 25 working boards — not by how interesting the idea is. Effort is honest: an XL/medium ranks below an S/high.
